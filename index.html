StarWish Kids

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

中文版 ↓

StarWish Kids is a star-reward chart that families keep on their own devices. It is designed for children, and it is built so that we never see your family's data.

We collect nothing

StarWish Kids does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-up, no email address, and no login.

Specifically, the app contains:

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks.
  • No servers of ours. We operate no backend and receive no data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — children's names, avatars, tasks, wish-list rewards, star totals and daily records — is stored:

  1. On your device, in the app's private storage, and
  2. In your own iCloud account, in Apple's CloudKit private database, if you are signed in to iCloud.

The iCloud copy exists so the same family data appears on your other Apple devices. It sits in your iCloud account and counts against your iCloud storage. We cannot read it, and neither can any other user of the app. Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app works normally and keeps everything on the device only.

Photos

You may optionally choose a photo from your photo library as a child's avatar. That photo is resized, saved with the child's profile, and synced only through your own iCloud account as described above. The app cannot browse your photo library: iOS shows the picker, and only the single image you choose is handed to the app. Photos are never uploaded anywhere else.

Children's privacy

StarWish Kids is intended to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian.

Because the app collects no personal information at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children, and there is nothing for us to disclose, retain, or delete on our side. This is by design and is intended to satisfy the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and comparable rules such as the GDPR provisions covering children.

The names you enter are labels for the chart. You are free to use nicknames or initials.

Parental controls

The "Parents" tab is protected by a passcode you set. Managing profiles, adjusting star totals, exporting or restoring backups, and changing the passcode all sit behind it. The passcode is stored in your device's Keychain and never leaves the device.

Backups you create

The app can export a backup file. That file is written wherever you choose — typically iCloud Drive or the Files app — and is entirely under your control. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete an individual child's profile from Parents → tap the child → Delete. This removes it from your devices and from your iCloud account.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on that device. Data in your iCloud account can be removed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes.

隐私政策

English ↑

StarWish Kids 是一款完全保存在你自己设备上的儿童星星奖励表。它为孩子设计，并且从架构上就保证我们看不到你家庭的任何数据。

我们不收集任何信息

StarWish Kids 不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享任何个人信息。App 没有账号体系，无需注册，不需要邮箱，也没有登录。

具体来说，App 中：

  • 没有任何统计或追踪。
  • 没有广告，也没有广告标识符。
  • 没有任何第三方 SDK，只使用 Apple 自家框架。
  • 我们没有服务器，不运营任何后端，也不接收任何数据。

你的数据存在哪里

你输入的一切——孩子的名字、头像、任务、心愿礼物、星星总数和每日记录——保存在：

  1. 你的设备上，位于 App 的私有存储空间；以及
  2. 你自己的 iCloud 账号中，即 Apple CloudKit 的私有数据库（前提是你已登录 iCloud）。

iCloud 那份副本的作用是让同一份家庭数据出现在你的其他 Apple 设备上。它位于你的 iCloud 账号内，占用你的 iCloud 存储空间。我们无法读取，App 的其他用户也无法读取。Apple 对该数据的处理遵循 Apple 隐私政策。

如果你没有登录 iCloud，App 一样正常使用，所有数据只保存在本机。

关于照片

你可以选择从相册中挑一张照片作为孩子的头像。该照片会被压缩后与孩子档案一起保存，并按上述方式仅通过你自己的 iCloud 账号同步。App 无法浏览你的相册：系统弹出选择器，只有你选中的那一张图片会交给 App。照片不会被上传到任何其他地方。

儿童隐私

StarWish Kids 应由家长或监护人设置和监督使用。

由于 App 完全不收集个人信息，我们不会在知情或不知情的情况下收集儿童信息，我们这边也不存在任何需要披露、保留或删除的数据。这一设计意在满足《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA）以及 GDPR 中涉及儿童的相关条款。

你输入的名字只是表格上的标签，完全可以使用昵称或缩写。

家长控制

「家长管理」页由你设置的密码保护。管理档案、调整星星总数、导出或恢复备份、修改密码都在这道门之后。密码保存在设备的 Keychain 中，绝不离开本机。

你自己创建的备份

App 可以导出备份文件。该文件写入你选定的位置——通常是 iCloud 云盘或「文件」App——完全由你掌控。我们永远不会收到它。

删除你的数据

  • 删除单个孩子的档案：家长管理 → 点击孩子卡片 → 删除这个档案。该操作会同时从你的设备和 iCloud 账号中移除。
  • 删除 App 可清除该设备上的全部数据。iCloud 账号中的数据可在设置 → [你的姓名] → iCloud → 管理账户存储空间中移除。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，新版本将发布在本页面并更新顶部日期。由于 App 不收集任何数据，我们预计不会有实质性变更。
